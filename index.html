About me

Alinda Bhattacharjee is a BIM Engineer and construction technology researcher. At HawarIT, he works on Revit-based HVAC/MEP design and prefabrication for Dutch residential and commercial projects (NEN/ISSO), and automates BIM workflows using Dynamo and pyRevit.

His research experience covers UAV photogrammetry, point-cloud processing, BIM-based progress monitoring and AI-driven construction applications. He is interested in LLMs, digital twins and DfMA to develop intelligent and automated construction workflows.

Complete HVAC & Plumbing Design for a Single-Family Residential Building

Complete HVAC and plumbing design and development for a single-family house, carried out by Alinda Bhattacharjee from the architectural information supplied for the project. The work covered the engineering calculations, system layout and spatial coordination required to turn that input into an installable design, delivered as a coordinated BIM model and IFC file.

HVAC design

Ventilation
Ventilation balance, airflow and restriction-ring calculations; duct sizing and routing; valve placement.
Heating
Heat-loss calculations to determine the required heat-pump capacity, followed by the principal scheme and selection of the ventilation unit on the calculated demand.
Technical room
Layout planned for distribution boxes, equipment, routing and installation access within the available space.
Acoustics
Acoustic performance was considered in the system design, with attention to applicable NEN/ISO requirements and the intended noise limits.

Plumbing design

Water supply
Water-flow calculations and domestic water distribution.
Drainage
Sewage capacity calculations and drainage routing.
Coordination
Pipe routes fitted within the available architectural and technical space, with installation access and the building layout considered throughout.

PVT solar integration

The building also incorporates a PVT solar panel system, which required the associated piping network to be engineered and coordinated with the overall installation. Bronze piping was used for this part of the system.

BIM & coordination

All systems were developed in a BIM workflow and coordinated against the architectural model, so the model reflects real space, routing and installation requirements, not calculation output alone. The coordinated model was delivered as IFC.

Prefabricated modular timber single-family houses: technical basis for HVAC and plumbing installation modelling

Alinda Bhattacharjee
Compiled from the manufacturer’s 2025 technical specification · Client, product and project names withheld

Summary

This note documents the technical parameters that governed the HVAC and plumbing installation models produced for 50+ prefabricated single-family houses in the Netherlands. The houses are factory-built timber-frame dwellings with cross-laminated timber (CLT) upper floors and a prefabricated concrete ground floor, delivered to site at finished level. Ten ground-bound variants in two typologies (one-storey and two-storey, both with pitched roofs) span 80–155 m² gross floor area. All dwellings are all-electric: a heat pump supplies underfloor heating and domestic hot water via a buffer vessel, ventilation is balanced with heat recovery, and roof-integrated PV supplies on-site generation. Design targets include envelope Rc values of 6.3 / 4.7 / 4.0 m²K/W (roof / façade / floor), airtightness qv;10 ≤ 0.4 dm³/s·m², energy label A+++ or better and an environmental performance (MPG) below 0.5. The models were coordinated to Dutch NEN and ISSO requirements.

Keywords: prefabrication; modular timber housing; CLT; all-electric; heat pump; balanced ventilation with heat recovery; BENG; NEN 1010; ISSO; installation modelling; DfMA

Technical note · Service · Heat-loss calculation

Heat-loss calculation for HVAC system sizing: room-by-room heating and cooling demand in Vabi Elements

Alinda Bhattacharjee
Vabi Elements · NEN-EN 12831 · ISSO 51 / 53 / 57

Summary

I carry out room-by-room heat-loss calculations in Vabi Elements to determine the heating capacity each room needs, as a traceable basis for HVAC system sizing. Each calculation takes into account geometry and orientation, construction Rc-values, window and door U-values, ventilation, infiltration, indoor and outdoor design temperatures, thermal bridges and system losses. Calculations follow NEN-EN 12831 and ISSO 51 / 53 / 57.

Keywords: heat-loss calculation; heating demand; cooling load; Vabi Elements; NEN-EN 12831; ISSO 51 / 53 / 57; Rc-value; U-value; HVAC system sizing
